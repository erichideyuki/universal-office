# uo-docx D1a reader — frontier R5: declarations

Part of the record `core/docs/modules/uo-docx.md` ("Milestone 1 — D1a foundation", brief §B). One
table row per site; every row names the TS file:line. Item 9 rows need an Opus check before they land.

Scope: `src/read/fulldoc.rs` ← `fulldoc.ts` (all of it except `markSectionBreaks`, which is the
session's, A5), `src/read/inspect.rs` ← `inspect.ts`, `src/read/blank.rs` ← `blank.ts`,
`src/read/fonts.rs` ← `fonts.ts` (`deobfuscateFont` is `uo_fonts::odttf::deobfuscate`). Public API:

- `fulldoc`: `read_document` (also `read::read_document`, replacing the foundation's stub),
  `read_blocks_of`, `read_blocks_grouped`, `read_textbox_content` (the block reader R4b's
  `read_textbox_content` calls: `BlocksMode::Blocks` = `readBlocks`, `Grouped` = `readBlocks` +
  `readBlocksGrouped`), `read_table`, `read_header_footer_part` (with `xml0`), `read_body_fragment`,
  `read_section_props`, `read_core_props`, `link_vml_textboxes`, `note_ref_ids`, `block_src`, and the
  `BlockSink` trait (a `Story`, or a `Vec<OwnedBlock>` when the caller needs the blocks one child
  produced);
- `inspect`: `document_is_empty`, `document_links` (+ `DocumentLink`), `document_custom_inlines`,
  `ParagraphsOf` (the TS generators `paragraphsOf`/`tableParagraphs` as one iterator), `units_have_text`;
- `blank`: `blank_docx_package`, `BlankDocxOptions`, `is_language_tag`, `BLANK_STYLES_XML`;
- `fonts`: `read_embedded_fonts` (re-exported from `read`).

Additive changes to shared files (none changes a model type): `ReadCtx::suspend_part`/`resume_part`
(the per-part paragraph and run counts are put aside while a header/footer part is read in the middle
of the body) and `ReadCtx::xml_text` (XML given as text: the editor's header, the body fragment) in
`ctx.rs`; `ReadContext::source_part()` (the getter of what `set_source_part` sets) in `paragraph.rs`;
the one match arm of R4b's `read_textbox_content` in `inline_objects.rs`; `read_document` and
`read_embedded_fonts` in `mod.rs`; the `fn` op of `js/regex-diff.mjs` (a TS expression that is more than
one regex); `Gen` exported from `js/quirk-sources.mjs`. Two corrections outside the frontier: R4c's
`ReadContext::new` and `ReadCtx::rels` read an **empty** relationships part as an error where the TS
(`if (relsXml)`) reads none (found by the first quirk replay, input `rel-empty-part`; both now use
`parts::part_doc`), and `tests/inline_objects_unit.rs` asserted the unwired block reader (now asserts the
story the wired one gives).

The shape of the port:

- the TS `Block[]` of a container is a `Story` (A2); `readBlocksOf`, `unwrap`, `unwrapRaw`,
  `readBlocksWithSections`, `forEachTextbox` and the inspect generators are loops over explicit stacks;
- the closures of `readFullDocument` (`onBookmark`/`onBookmarkEnd`, `readHfSet`, `closeSection`) are the
  local state of `read_document`: the paragraph reader queues the bookmark marks (R4c's
  `bookmark_events`) and the read drains them after each body child, before the child's blocks are
  pushed (`blocks.length` then is the TS value); a header/footer part read in the middle of the body
  has its own `ReadContext` (`buildReadContext` per part) over the same `ReadCtx`;
- the order of everything that allocates a value (`SourceKey`s, `wrapIds`, `blockSdtKeys`) is the TS
  order: the body's story is registered in `stories` first, header/footer parts are read at the
  `closeSection` that names them, notes after the comments, then styles, formatting, core props and the
  background;
- parts that are absent or empty read as nothing (`getPartText` is `undefined` or `''`): the part check
  of `parts::part_doc`; a missing `word/document.xml`, a `w:document` without `w:body` and an empty
  document part are errors (`DocxError`), as the TS throws.
